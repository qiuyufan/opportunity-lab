Evidence inbox

Collect raw signals before interpreting them: complaints, reviews, forum posts, search behavior, competitor weaknesses, and interview observations.

Evidence ≠ idea.Record what actually happened or what a real person said. You can ask AI to cluster it later.

Problem log

Turn repeated evidence into clear problem statements. Do not write the solution yet.

Customer interviews

Ask about past behavior. Avoid “Would you use my app?”

Rank the problems

Score only after collecting evidence. 1 = weak, 5 = strong.

Active hypothesis

Turn your strongest problem into a falsifiable business hypothesis.

AI Copilot

Generate evidence-grounded prompts from what you have collected. Copy the prompt into ChatGPT or another AI tool, then save the useful analysis below.

No synthetic evidence.The generated prompts explicitly tell AI to distinguish facts from assumptions and to use only the material you collected.

Paid validation test

Offer the smallest useful outcome manually before building software.

Weekly review

Make one evidence-based decision: continue, narrow, retest, or drop it.

Decision rule

Do not continue because you already spent time on it. Continue because the evidence became stronger.

Interview prompts

Keep these visible during conversations.

Tell me about the last time you tried to do this.
Past behavior is more reliable than hypothetical intent.
What was the hardest part?
How do you solve it today?
How much time or money does that cost?
What happens if you do nothing?
Have you paid for anything to solve it?
